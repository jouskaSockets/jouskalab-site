Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

JouskaLab is a personal tool with a single user: its owner.

  • It accesses only the files it creates in the owner's own Google Drive (the drive.file permission).
  • Data goes only between the owner's computer and the owner's Google Drive. It is not sent to, shared with, or sold to anyone else.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • Access can be removed at any time in the Google Account under Security, Third-party connections.
